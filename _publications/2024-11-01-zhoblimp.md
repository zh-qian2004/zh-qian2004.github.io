Established the ZhoBLiMP benchmark, a Minimal Pair Paradigm (MPP)-based resource for the systematic evaluation of Chinese linguistic phenomena. Proposed the SLLN-LP metric to eliminate sentence length bias, achieving robust evaluation performance across multiple MPP-based benchmarks. Trained the Zh-Pythia model suite to analyze models' acquisition of Chinese syntax.

**Key Contributions:**
(1) Constructed the templates for generating linguistic minimal pairs;
(2) Preprocessed the large-scale pretraining corpus for Zh-Pythia model suite.
