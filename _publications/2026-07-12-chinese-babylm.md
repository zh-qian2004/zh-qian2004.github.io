Co-organized the first ChineseBabyLM Challenge, organized as part of NLPCC 2026. The challenge asked participants to train language models from scratch using no more than 102M Chinese words, with no restrictions on tokenizers, architectures, or training epochs. Models were evaluated on three tracks: natural language understanding, cognitive alignment, and Hanzi knowledge. Eighteen teams submitted 28 distinct models, with the overall-winning team using a DeBERTa-v2 architecture with an auxiliary pinyin-prediction objective.

**Key Contributions:**
(1) Co-organized the challenge and contributed to the design of its evaluation tracks;
(2) Helped establish a benchmark for advancing data-efficient and cognitively plausible approaches to Chinese language modeling.
