Analyzed the temporal organization of prenuclear glides [j] and [w] in Hefei Mandarin. Employed acoustic measurements and RSD analysis on syllables. Revealed that both glides exhibit a rightward shift and lack C-centering effects, indicating they function as part of the rime rather than forming complex onsets.

**Key Contributions:**
(1) Performed detailed phonetic annotation for the Hefei Mandarin dataset;
(2) Executed temporal and acoustic analyses guided by the C-centering framework and the DQC model.
