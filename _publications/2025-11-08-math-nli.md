Benchmarked diverse LMs on two expert-compiled mathematical NLI datasets. Results uncovered that while models robustly detect contradictions, they significantly struggle with inferring neutral relationships.

*Note: First author is corresponding; others in alphabetical order by last name.*

**Key Contributions:**
Conducted a systematic evaluation of 10 SOTA LLMs on our MathNLI dataset, identifying specific failure modes in mathematical reasoning tasks.
